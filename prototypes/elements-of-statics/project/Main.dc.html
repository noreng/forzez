The Elements of Statics

Axioms.

I. Two forces hold a body in balance when they are equal, opposite, and on one line.

II. Two forces at a point act as one: the diagonal of the parallelogram they span.

III. A balanced set of forces may be added or taken away; the body never notices.

IV. Whatever one body does to another, the other does back: equal, and opposite.

Everything that follows is built from these four, and from nothing else.
