Proposition I. Theorem.

If three forces, not parallel, hold a body in balance, their lines of action meet in one point; and drawn one after another, the three forces close a triangle.

+ = (Ax. II)
+ = 0, for the body is in balance.
Therefore lies on the line of (Ax. I) and passes through the point where and meet.
And placed tip to tail, end where they began.
