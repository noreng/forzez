Book IV · Trusses Prop. VI

Proposition VI. Theorem.

Reverse every load on a truss, and every member keeps its size but changes its sense: red and blue trade places, and nothing else changes.

Every joint is three forces closing a triangle (Book I, Prop. I). Turn every arrow round, and each triangle still closes, with the same sides.

So each member keeps its size, and only its sense flips: ⇄

Corollary. A roof under snow and the same roof under wind suction see the same numbers, with the colours swapped. A rafter that only ever learned to push may not enjoy being pulled.
