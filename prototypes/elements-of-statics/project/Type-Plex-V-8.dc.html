Book V · Cables and arches Proposition 8 · theorem

An arch and a chain, tied together, cancel their thrust.

Tie an arch and a chain of the same shape to the same two ends, and join them with posts. The arch pushes out as hard as the chain pulls in, so the supports feel no thrust at all.

1 Both curves are the string polygon of equal loads. (II.5, V.3)
2 The posts hand half of every load to the chain.
3 out = in, so the thrust stays inside.
