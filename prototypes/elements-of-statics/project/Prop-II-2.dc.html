Proposition II. Theorem.

About any point, the turning of a resultant equals the turnings of its parts together. Each turning is a triangle with its apex at the point.

+ =

All three triangles share the side from the pivot to the start of the forces. On that side, their heights add just as the forces do (Ax. II), so their areas add too.

Corollary. Split a force any way you like; the turning about any point stays the same.
