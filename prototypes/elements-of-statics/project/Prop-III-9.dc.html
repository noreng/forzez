Book III · Bodies and supports Prop. IX

Proposition IX. Theorem.

Three supports hold a body fast, unless their three lines meet in one point. Then the body may turn about that point, and nothing in the supports can stop it.

Each roller can push only along its own line: All three pass through the black point, so about that point not one of them has an arm (Book II, Prop. I).

Any load that would turn the plate about the point finds no answer. Three supports, three numbers, and still a mechanism.

Corollary. Three parallel lines meet too, at infinity: a plate on three level rollers slides away sideways. Counting supports is not enough; their lines must be looked at.
