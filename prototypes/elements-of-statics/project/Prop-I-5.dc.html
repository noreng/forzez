Proposition V. Theorem.

When three forces at a point are in balance, each is as the sine of the angle between the other two.

: sin = : sin = : sin

The three forces close a triangle (Prop. I). Each corner of that triangle is what its wedge leaves of a straight angle, and an angle and what it leaves have the same sine. The rule of sines in the triangle does the rest.

Each force is painted on the angle it does not touch. Bernard Lamy, 1687.
