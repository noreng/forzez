Book II · Forces in the plane Prop. III

Proposition III. Theorem.

Two equal, opposite, parallel forces have no resultant; they only turn. Their turning is the rectangle of one force and the distance between them, wherever the pair is placed.

+ = nothing that pushes, for they are equal and opposite;
yet not on one line (Ax. I fails), so they leave a turn: =

Slide the pair along the body, turn it, or trade force for distance: as long as the rectangle keeps its size, the body cannot tell the difference.

Every steering wheel and every screwdriver knows this proposition.
