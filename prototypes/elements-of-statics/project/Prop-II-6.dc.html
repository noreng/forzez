Book II · Forces in the plane Prop. VI

Proposition VI. Problem.

To find the centre of gravity of any flat body: hang it twice, from two points, and draw the plumb line each time. Where the lines cross is the centre.

At rest, the body feels only two forces: the pin holding it up and its own weight. By Ax. I they lie on one line, so the weight hangs straight below the pin, along the plumb line.

The centre is somewhere on and somewhere on ; two lines meet once.

Corollary. A third hanging adds nothing but reassurance: its line passes through the same point.
