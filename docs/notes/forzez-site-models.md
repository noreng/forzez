# Forzez — site & business models to learn from (2026-10-26)

Companion to `forzez-roadmap.md`, `elements-of-statics.md` (KW benchmark lives there too).
Question: how do small, purpose-driven science/design projects build a brand AND make some money? What can forzez.com take?

## Catalogue structure (proposal, 2026-10-26)
Two axes: **what** (content lines) × **how** (formats). Everything on the site sits in one cell.

### Content lines (the "what")
- **Studio** — generative art. Organised in **Series** (Series 01 — Arches, 02 — Trusses, 03 — Force Polygons, later Tension / Compression / Equilibrium). One piece = **Sheet No. 017**, with a Forze Code.
- **Editions** — the books. No. 1 *The Elements of Statics*, No. 2 *The Beauty of Trusses*. Inside: Books → Propositions → **Plates** ("Plate 5.3").
- **Custom** — the configurator and personal pieces (name as truss, your bridge, your date). Output is a Sheet with its own Forze Code.
- **Places** — real structures (Lánchíd, Szabadság híd, Forth Bridge…). Can live as a Series in Studio rather than its own line.

### Formats (the "how")
| Format | What it is | Made by | Price idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | web plates, PDF plate download, wallpapers, "watch it draw" | site | €0 |
| Postcard | small plotted card, the Friends perk and order insert | own plotter | in membership / free with orders |
| Print | open edition, archival | POD (Prodigi/Gelato) | €20–40 |
| Original | pen-plotted, numbered (e.g. /25), signed | family plotter station | €100+ |
| Book | booklets in a slipcase | Kickstarter + printer | €60–150 tiers |

### Which cells exist
| | Free | Postcard | Print | Original | Book |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Studio (Series) | watch it draw, wallpaper | yes | yes | yes — the core | no |
| Editions (Plates) | all plates on the web | yes | per-Book posters + axioms poster | selected plates only | yes — the core |
| Custom | configurator + SVG | no | yes | yes (premium) | no |

### Vocabulary rules
- Only four product words: **Postcard · Print · Original · Book.** "Poster" = a Print of an Editions plate (say "Plate 5.3, Print").
- "Series" belongs to Studio only. Editions use Book / Proposition / Plate.
- Every physical piece carries an identifier: Sheet No. + Forze Code (Studio, Custom) or Plate number (Editions).

### Cross-links (what makes it one site)
- Plate → the Studio pieces built on the same principle ("see it as art"). Inverted chain ↔ Series 01 Arches; force polygons ↔ Series 03.
- Studio piece → "the theorem behind it" (its plate).
- Every page → newsletter.

### Navigation
Studio · Editions · Make your own · Shop · Notes · About. Shop filters by format (Print / Original / Book), not by line.

### Fulfilment
- POD for Prints (no inventory), plotter station for Originals and postcards, printer + Kickstarter for the Book, static site for Free.

## The models

### 1. Abakcus (abakcus.com) — the calm curated hub
- One person: Ali Kaya, mathematician/curator, dad, wrote on Medium for years before the site.
- Sections: Articles · Books · Videos · DIY · Tools · Gadgets + weekly newsletter "Beyond Euclid" (Substack, 237 issues).
- Voice: first person, slow, "picked slowly", no hype. Every card has a one-sentence hook that is itself interesting.
- Money: "No ads. Ever." + Patreon ($1/month framed as "buy me a tea"). Books/gadgets links may be affiliate — not verified.
- No shop of its own. Brand = taste + consistency + newsletter.
- **Already covers our territory**: a Byrne Euclid book page, "68 Best Geometry Books" (starts with Byrne), and a full Kronecker Wallis catalog article. → Ali is a natural first press/newsletter contact for The Elements of Statics.

### 2. Kronecker Wallis (kroneckerwallis.com) — the object publisher
- Founded 2016 in Barcelona by a few people; founder/editor Jordi Anton. Name = two mathematicians.
- Model: complete classic text + best translation + design worthy of it + often hand binding. Runs of 90–1,000 copies; some sewn and numbered to order.
- Kickstarter is the launcher: Principia first (founded the press), then Euclid, Humboldt, Tesla.
- Euclid campaign: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1174653512/euclids-elements-completing-oliver-byrnes-work
  - Aug 22 – Sep 12, 2017 (21 days), €258,139 of €115,000, 1,560 backers, avg ~€165, "Project We Love".
  - 883 of 1,560 backers from the US (top cities: SF, London, NYC). → write in English, price in EUR, ship worldwide.
  - Book now €200, sold out, reprint mid-2027. Posters: one per book + cover.
- Same creator account for Tesla: kickstarter.com/projects/1174653512/…
- Price ladder: €20 (small A5) → €40–80 (most books) → €200 (flagship). Posters as the cheap entry.
- Where the audience came from: not their own channels (FB ~435 followers). Famous subjects (Newton, Byrne's cult following via Tufte/Taschen), design + maths press during live campaigns (Aperiodical covered Principia in 2016; Euclid got It's Nice That, Fast Company, The Verge, Core77), Kickstarter's "Project We Love", and Principia backers seeding Euclid. They built the list campaign by campaign.
- Weaknesses to avoid: years-long sold-out gaps, slow dispatch, no owned audience.

### 3. Nicholas Rougeux / c82.net — the free interactive edition
- Free web reproduction of Byrne's Euclid with interactive diagrams and cross-references; posters sold in sizes.
- Press: Colossal, Open Culture, The Aperiodical. Content free (public domain), posters + site design are his.
- His About page lists other Byrne reproductions (Taschen, KW) → a place to get listed later.
- Lesson: the free interactive version IS the marketing for the paid print.

### 4. The Public Domain Review — membership + prints
- Not-for-profit since 2011. "Friends" = annual donors (from ~$40/yr) get twice-yearly themed postcard packs, 15% off prints, name on an honour page.
- Prints shop since 2017 (giclée, from $40). PDR Press: annual "Selected Essays". Bookshop.org affiliate shelf.
- Donations ~$140k/yr — "only just enough". Lesson: membership with a physical perk beats a bare tip jar.

### 5. Practical Engineering (Grady Hillhouse) — engineer → channel → book
- Civil engineer, ~10 years consulting, then YouTube full time (millions of subs).
- Book: *Engineering in Plain Sight* (No Starch Press, 2022), on Bill Gates' 2024 holiday list. Signed copies via own store.
- Lesson: an engineer explaining the built world has a big audience; the book came after the audience, through a publisher.

### 6. Pen-plotter artists (Forzez Originals tier)
- Michelle Chandra (Dirt Alley Design): plots + riso prints of the same designs, postcards included with orders, blog as funnel, AxiDraw affiliate codes.
- Joel Dare's Pen Art Shop: series of 25 unique plotted variations at $25, free printable for email signup.
- Others to study: Dan Catt, Adam Fuhrer, Lia, Simon Kirby, Amy Goodchild, Nadieh Bremer (data-viz prints + plots), lostpixels (plotted vs printed).
- Evil Mad Scientist "featured artist" posts = plotter-community press.

### 7. Bauhaus Posters (bauhausposters.com) — the decor poster shop (researched 2026-10-26)
- Paris company (SAS), founded 2019 ("exactly 100 years after the Bauhaus"). Anonymous "team of artists"; no named makers, no founder story.
- Product: original geometric prints "in the spirit of" the Bauhaus (not reproductions), ~100 in the primary-colour collection alone. 6 sizes, optional solid-wood frame (oak / black ash / white ash).
- Fulfilment: 96 partner printers in 24 countries, printed and framed near the buyer, 1–2 days production, 51 countries. Pure POD network (looks like Gelato's model — not verified).
- Spec as marketing: pigment giclée, FSC paper, "100-year colour guarantee", 5-step craft page. Turns POD into "museum grade".
- Shopping UX: browse by colour collection, by style, by room ("which style for which space"), colour pairings. Sold as interior decor, not as ideas.
- Channels: Pinterest only + weekly email (1,720 subscribers). Journal = generic SEO articles on Bauhaus history.
- Numbers they publish: 3,400+ orders, 6,500+ prints, 350+ reviews (4.9/5) over ~7 years → roughly 500 orders/year. A real but modest business.
- Pricing behaviour: constant promotions ("−40% final flash sale", "gallery offer up to −20%", free shipping over €60). Discounting is the engine.
- **What Forzez takes:** POD + framing as the Print tier (validated: originals sell without inventory); the spec/craft page; Pinterest as a channel for geometric decor; size + frame options; filter by colour/room for the Print shop.
- **What Forzez avoids:** anonymity (our edge is the engineer + family + story); generic SEO journal; permanent discounting (cheapens Originals and the Kickstarter); "Bauhaus-looking poster" as the pitch. That market is crowded with pretty geometry. Forzez must sell what they can't: the physics decided the composition, every line carries a real force, Forze Code reproduces it.
- Reality check: an anonymous, well-run decor shop with ~100 designs did ~3,400 orders in 7 years with 1.7k email subscribers. Decor alone is a slow grind; story + press (KW) is the multiplier.

## What Forzez takes (decisions to make, not yet decided)

### Site shape (Abakcus calm + KW objects + c82 interactivity + Bauhaus Posters' print shop mechanics)
- **Elements of Statics** — free web plates (interactive later), one per page, signup on each. Lives on forzez.com (decided 2026-10-26); proposed under a "Forzez Editions" imprint.
- **The Beauty of Trusses** — second volume, same visual language.
- **Studio** (generative art) — seeded pieces, Forze Code, "watch it draw".
- **Notes** — short essays: Byrne, Maxwell, Culmann, Kármán, Szabadság híd, the spaghetti bridge. Abakcus-style one-line hooks.
- **Shop** — Prints (incl. plate posters, sizes + frames, POD near the buyer), plotted Originals; later the book.
- **Newsletter** — the spine of it all (KW's Kickstarter needed a list; Abakcus' brand is its newsletter).
- Voice: first person, one engineer + family, dry humour, sentence case, no ads.

### Revenue ladder (cheap → premium)
1. Free: plates, articles, configurator.
2. Newsletter (free) — target a few thousand before any Kickstarter.
3. "Friends of Forzez" (~€5/month or ~€40/yr): plotted postcard pack twice a year (the plotter makes this cheap and unique), discount, name on a page.
4. Prints (€20–40, framed higher): Axioms poster, one per Book, generative Prints via POD.
5. Plotted Originals (€100+): numbered, signed, small series (25). Never discounted.
6. The book: Kickstarter preorder (booklets in a slipcase), €60–150 tiers.

### Press map (in order)
1. Abakcus / Beyond Euclid (Ali covers Byrne + KW already).
2. The Aperiodical, Open Culture, Colossal, It's Nice That, Fast Company Co.Design, kottke.
3. c82 (get listed among Byrne-style works), Evil Mad Scientist (plotter side).
4. Engineering side: Practical Engineering community, structural-engineering educators (Truzme contacts — but keep Truzme school-safe).
5. Hacker News for the interactive plate engine.
6. Pinterest (organic, decor audience) for Prints — Bauhaus Posters' only social channel.

## How to find more examples
- Follow the citation graph: whoever covers Byrne/KW/c82 covers this niche. Read their "related" lists.
- Kickstarter: Publishing + Art Books + Design categories; search "visual", "illustrated", "mathematics", "Byrne". Check numbers on Kicktraq / BackerTracker (daily backers, avg pledge).
- Abakcus' own Tools/Books/Gadgets sections = a curated list of exactly these makers.
- Patreon search: "mathematics", "engineering", "interactive explanations".
- Search patterns: "<classic text> redesign kickstarter", "interactive explanation patreon", "pen plotter shop edition", "science poster shop independent", "visual explanation book crowdfunded".
- Shop "About" pages often publish order counts and review counts (Bauhaus Posters did) → cheap way to size a niche.
- For each find, note: who, audience, free product, paid product, price ladder, launch channel, list size if visible.

## Expectation check
- KW's €258k = Euclid's brand power + 2017 design-press moment. Statics is smaller; plan for a fraction.
- Bauhaus Posters: ~500 orders/year from a pure decor shop. Decor without story grows slowly.
- Time budget is evenings/weekends. Sequence: list first, posters second, Kickstarter only with ~15–20 plates done.
