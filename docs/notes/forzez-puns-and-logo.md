## Manifesto line (added 2026-10-17)
- **Form follows Forzez.** — twist on "form (ever) follows function" (Louis Sullivan, 1896; the modernist / Bauhaus motto).
- Literally true: in form-finding (spider web, soap film, Gaudí's hanging chain) the shape *is* the result of the forces.
- Use the singular "Form follows…" to mirror the original exactly (more recognisable than "Forms follow…").
- Role: **brand manifesto** — About page headline, title block / back of prints, Product Hunt tagline. "Forzez of nature" stays the playful everyday line.
- Small print for the About page: *"Sullivan said form follows function. We'd add: function follows forces."*

## Logo (3 variants made, thick-stroke Bauhaus)
Colour meaning (decided 2026-10-13): red = compression, blue = tension, yellow = load.
1. **Truss Z's** — the two Z's are truss panels: black chords + pins, first diagonal red (compression), second blue (tension), O = yellow disc. → **main wordmark** (header, prints).
2. **ZEZ ligature** — Z-E-Z fused into one truss with shared chords; E stem = post, yellow mid-arm = load. Clever but reads "FORZ-Z" at first glance → keep for a limited-series special mark.
3. **Badge** — one truss-Z in a big yellow circle, plain wordmark below. → **icon** (Instagram, favicon, stamp, packaging).
